Description

AF/ArmaFlex Evo 2m Lengths - The Evolution of Elastomeric Insulation

AF/ArmaFlex Evo 2m lengths mark the next generation of elastomeric insulation, replacing the renowned ArmaFlex Class 0 with superior fire safety, condensation control, and energy efficiency. As Armacell’s 8th-generation product, this flexible, closed-cell foam insulation outperforms its predecessor, offering enhanced smoke suppression and antimicrobial protection. Pre-cut to 2m lengths, it ensures seamless installation for HVAC, refrigeration, and process equipment applications.

Technical Specifications

  • Thermal Conductivity: Tubes (7-25 mm thickness): λ₀ ≤ 0.033 W/(m·K); Tubes (25-45 mm): λ₀ ≤ 0.036 W/(m·K), optimizing energy savings (EN ISO 13787, EN ISO 8497).
  • Water Vapour Diffusion Resistance: Tubes (7-25 mm): μ ≥ 10,000; Tubes (>25 mm): μ ≥ 7,000, ensuring robust condensation control (EN 12086, EN 13469).
  • Fire Performance: B/BL-s2,d0 rating with 50% less smoke than Class 0, enhancing evacuation safety (EN 13501-1, EN 13823). Self-extinguishing and non-dripping.
  • Temperature Range: -50°C to +110°C, ideal for hot and cold piping systems (EN 14706, EN 14707).
  • Antimicrobial Protection: Built-in Microban® technology prevents mould, mildew, and bacteria, a new feature not standard in Class 0 (EN ISO 846, VDI 6022).
  • Dimensions: Suits pipe diameters 6-168 mm, thicknesses 7-45 mm, meeting EN 14304 tolerances (EN 822, EN 823).
  • Fire Resistance: EI 30 to EI 90 ratings for fire-stopping applications (EN 13501-2, EN 1366-3).

Key Features

  • Surpasses Class 0: Unlike ArmaFlex Class 0 (typically B-s3,d0), AF/ArmaFlex Evo achieves a B/BL-s2,d0 rating, reducing smoke emission by 50% for safer buildings.
  • Advanced Safety: Low smoke (s2) and no flaming droplets (d0) improve visibility and evacuation time, a significant upgrade over older Armacell insulations.
  • System Compatibility: Pairs with ArmaFlex 525 or 520 adhesives and accessories like ArmaFlex Protect for a reliable insulation system.
  • Effortless Installation: Lightweight, flexible 2m tubes, with optional self-adhesive closures for faster fitting compared to Class 0’s standard options.
  • Sustainable Design: Type III Environmental Product Declaration (EPD) certified, dust- and fibre-free, with low chloride ions (≤ 300 ppm), aligning with modern standards (ISO 14025, EN 15804).
  • Long-Term Durability: Maintains thermal and fire performance without degradation, outperforming earlier specifications (EN 14304).

Applications

Perfect for insulating pipes in refrigeration systems, air conditioning units, and hot or cold water pipelines, AF/ArmaFlex Evo 2m lengths excel in commercial, industrial, and residential settings. They prevent energy loss, protect against corrosion, and enhance system longevity with greater safety than Class 0.

Why Choose AF/ArmaFlex Evo?

AF/ArmaFlex Evo builds on Armacell’s 40-year legacy, surpassing ArmaFlex Class 0 with improved fire safety, Microban® antimicrobial protection, and higher vapour resistance. Its B/BL-s2,d0 rating and reduced smoke output make it a safer, more efficient choice for modern installations. Upgrade to Evo for insulation that sets a new standard in performance and protection.
